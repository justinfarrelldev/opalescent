Bounded cancellation design:
- CancellationSource::request() sets the sticky request sequence on the cancellation source state, then synchronizes with each watched wait set by locking and releasing that wait set's predicate mutex before Condvar::notify_all.
- SystemWaitSet::wait_sync() uses CancellationToken::request_sequence() as the sole authoritative sticky cancellation predicate and ordering sequence.
- WaitSetState does not retain cancellation generations/sequences; there is no per-wait-set cancellation_requests list, map, or vector.
- Already-published pending ready work with sequence older than cancellation drains first; after that sticky cancellation wins over newer level-ready or queued source work.
